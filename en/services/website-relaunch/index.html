Why a website relaunch is needed now

Old sites do not only look dated. They lose speed, security, mobile use and therefore enquiries.

Old design

Looks unintentionally cheap – regardless of the quality of your work.

Slow loading

Visitors will not wait. Neither will Google.

Weak mobile version

Text too small, menu unusable, form cut off.

Weak conversion

No visible CTA, only a mailto address, dead buttons.

Outdated technology

Insecure plugins, no forced HTTPS, no maintenance.

SEO migration, not just looks

Keep URLs

Important addresses stay where it makes sense.

Redirects

Old paths point to the new targets instead of becoming 404s.

Metadata

Title, description and canonical are reset, not forgotten.

Index and analytics

Sitemap, robots, Search Console and measurement stay part of the plan.

What should change after the relaunch

Trust

The site matches the quality of your work again.

Enquiries

CTA and form are visible – including on phones.

Calm in Google

Visibility is moved, not casually deleted.

SEO migration checklist

Important URLs

List of pages that have traffic or backlinks.

Redirects

Old paths → new targets, 1:1 where possible.

Metas

Do not leave titles and descriptions empty or duplicated.

Indexing

robots.txt, sitemap, canonical, Search Console.

Analytics

Keep measurement comparable before and after launch.

Website relaunch for Vienna businesses

Many Vienna companies have had the same site for years. Customers now expect speed, mobile and a form. A relaunch in Vienna means: look current, be reachable and keep the addresses Google already knows.

Why not a website builder or a cheap template?

Builders, Wix or a ready-made theme are fast. For a Vienna business that wants enquiries, structure, copy, tech and post-launch ownership are often missing.
